About

MIKEHIBYEOK is Michael Sullivan. Detroit.

He makes digital art, AI-assisted images, video, and mixed media. Some of it is slop. Some of it isn't. The filter tags at the top tell you which is which — or at least, what series it belongs to.

At the center of it all: a self-portrait photography series focused on hair, now 10 years and counting. The full archive is here on the site.

Selected exhibitions: shown as part of BEEPLE: INFINITE_LOOP — an immersive mid-career survey at Node Gallery, Palo Alto (2026); selected for Shepard Fairey: Obey & Resist at Beeple Studios, Charleston (2026); Portrait: Self and Others, PhotoPlace Gallery, juried by Aline Smithson (2024); A Celebration of Biodiversity, Insecta Collective CIC, Leicester, UK (2026).

He treats AI as a visible tool, not a hidden one. The process is part of the work.

This site is the running archive. Newest first. Everything labeled.
