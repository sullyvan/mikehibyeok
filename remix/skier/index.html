Skier Rooster — Quartier Alpin

2026 · Street View Remix — Detroit × Megève · Hybrid: photography + AI compositing · Michael Sullivan / MikeHiByeOK

English

A rooster and a skier from a vintage travel poster glide through the alpine quarter — folklore and play returned to the street where daily life actually happens.

From the series Google Street View Remix — Detroit × Megève: Street View used as both archive and canvas, remixed with the artist's own imagery and with echoes pulled from museums, memory, and the subconscious. These digital street remixes let hidden art — personal, cultural, or institutional — slip back into public space.
